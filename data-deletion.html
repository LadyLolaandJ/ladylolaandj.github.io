Data Deletion Instructions

Lady Lola and J Studio

Lady Lola and J Studio is a private application used by the owners of Lady Lola and J to manage content and their connected Facebook Page.

These instructions explain how to revoke Facebook access and request deletion of information retained by the Studio. See also our Privacy Policy.

1. Remove the application from Facebook

  1. Open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Find Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, depending on the interface.
  3. Locate Lady Lola and J Studio.
  4. Select Remove and follow Facebook’s instructions.

Removing the application revokes its access through Facebook. It does not automatically delete information already stored locally by the Studio.

2. Disconnect Facebook from the Studio

Open The Studio → Publishing Setup → Facebook and select Disconnect.

This removes the locally encrypted Facebook Page access token and the saved connection status.

Disconnecting does not automatically remove the saved Page identification record, historical publishing records, or the application-level Meta App Secret.

3. Request deletion of retained information

Email ladylolaandj@gmail.com with the subject:

Facebook data deletion request

We will verify the request as appropriate and review the Facebook-related information retained by the Studio.

Information eligible for deletion may include:

  • Saved Facebook Page connection details.
  • Facebook access credentials.
  • Historical publishing records and associated request logs.
  • Other locally retained Facebook-related information associated with the connection.

We will acknowledge the request and provide a status update or confirmation of deletion within 30 days.

If any information cannot be deleted immediately because of technical, legal, security, or backup-retention requirements, we will explain what remains and how it will be handled.

4. Backup copies

The Studio maintains backups for recovery purposes.

Scheduled backups are retained according to a rotating retention policy. Some backups may temporarily contain older copies of Facebook connection details or publishing records.

Manual backups may be retained separately and require individual review.

When processing a verified deletion request, we will identify applicable backup copies and delete them where feasible or ensure that deleted information is not reintroduced during a future restoration.

Encrypted Facebook access tokens and the Meta App Secret are stored separately from the scheduled database backups.

5. Information held by Meta

Removing information from Lady Lola and J Studio does not automatically delete information held independently by Meta.

Facebook posts already published on the Lady Lola and J Page are also separate from the Studio’s locally stored publishing records. Requests concerning published content can be sent to the contact address below.
