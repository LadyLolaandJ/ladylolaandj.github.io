Privacy Policy

Lady Lola and J Studio
Effective: November 23, 2026

1. Who we are and what this app is

Lady Lola and J is an affiliate-marketing and lifestyle brand based in Northwest Arkansas, United States. Lady Lola and J Studio (“the Studio”) is a private desktop application that we run on one Windows computer to prepare content and to manage publishing to our own Facebook Page, “Lady Lola and J.”

The Studio is not a public service. Only the owners of Lady Lola and J use it, and no one else can sign in to it. It does not have customers, members, or visitors.

2. Facebook data the Studio accesses

2.1 Through the Facebook connection

When an owner connects the Studio to Facebook, Facebook asks the owner to approve the permissions below. The Studio works with one Page only: Lady Lola and J.

PermissionWhat we use it for
pages_show_listTo list the Pages the connecting owner manages, so we can confirm that the Lady Lola and J Page is the one selected.
pages_manage_postsTo publish posts (a photo post or a video Reel) that the owners have approved, to the Lady Lola and J Page.
pages_manage_engagementTo add our own first comment (which holds our affiliate link) to a post we just published.
pages_read_engagementTo read back the Page and the posts we published (post ID, link, time and visibility) to confirm that a post went out correctly.

Facebook also grants public_profile and pages_read_user_content along with these permissions. The Studio does not use either one, and it does not store anything obtained through them.

If the connecting owner also manages other Pages or has a personal profile, the Studio does not store them, name them, or post to them.

Through this connection the Studio does not request or store follower lists, the names or profiles of people who like or comment, or private messages. After we add our own comment to a post, the Studio may read the comments on that same post only to check that ours is there. It does not save what other people wrote; it saves only a fingerprint of our own comment.

2.2 Performance figures (aggregate statistics)

We track how our posts perform, such as views, link clicks, interactions, and the total number of followers. These are aggregate counts: numbers for a Page or a post as a whole, for example “17 views” or “53 followers.” They do not identify any person.

  • These figures do not come through the Facebook connection described above. They come from Meta Business Suite and similar dashboards that we view while signed in to our own accounts. They are typed in by us or read from the dashboard pages in the Studio’s own browser window, which keeps only the numbers.
  • Those dashboards can show other people’s names, for example next to a comment. We do not save names, profiles, comment text, or follower lists in the Studio.
  • Aggregate figures are kept as business records. They are not personal data about any individual, and they are not removed by the deletion steps below.

4. How we protect it

  • Encryption. The Page token and the app secret are encrypted with Windows data protection (through the Electron “safeStorage” feature). This is designed to help protect them from being read by other Windows accounts on the computer and from being copied to and used on another computer.
  • Separate location. The encrypted files are kept outside the Studio’s data folder, outside our backups, and outside our project files.
  • Limited use of credentials. The token is decrypted only inside the Studio program when it is needed to contact Facebook. It is not shown in the Studio’s windows and is not sent to any service other than Facebook. The AI helpers described in section 6 are blocked from opening the folder that holds the encrypted files.
  • Approval before publishing. The Studio has a global publishing hold. A post can be sent to Facebook only after an owner has approved that exact post and has issued a one-time authorization for it.
No method of storage is perfect. Windows encryption does not protect against software or a person who is already signed in to our Windows account on our computer, so we also keep that computer secured.

5. How we use the data

Only to run our own Facebook Page: to confirm the right Page is connected, to publish posts we approved, to add our first comment, to confirm that a post is live, to understand how our posts perform in aggregate, and to keep an accurate record of what we published.

6. Who else receives it

  • Meta (Facebook). The Studio sends requests to Facebook’s servers to do the tasks above.
  • AI service (Anthropic’s Claude). The Studio uses Claude to help us research products, draft and check posts, and review results. When it runs, the AI helpers can read the Studio’s local records and can use the Studio’s own tools. As a result, Facebook-related information that may be sent to Claude includes: the Page name and ID, the connection status and permission names, our post drafts, publishing records and outcomes (post IDs and links), and our aggregate performance figures, as well as anything we type to the AI ourselves. It does not include the Page token or the app secret: the encrypted files are blocked from the AI helpers, and the decrypted values are never given to them. Anthropic handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Cloud backup storage. We keep backups of the Studio’s database and media on our computer and copy them to cloud storage we own (Microsoft OneDrive and Google Drive). These backups include the connection record, our post records and the request log. They do not include the encrypted token or app secret. We also copy our business records (such as our decision log and status notes) to the same storage. Those notes can mention the Page name and ID and the permission names. They never contain tokens or secrets.
  • Website hosting. These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits to them under its own policies. We do not run analytics or tracking on these pages.

We do not sell Facebook data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

You can remove the Studio’s access at any time from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy, Settings, Apps and Websites or Business Integrations). You can also ask us to delete the Facebook-related information the Studio retains by emailing ladylolaandj@gmail.com with the subject Facebook data deletion request. We will acknowledge the request and give a status update or a confirmation within 30 days. We do not guarantee that every backup copy is deleted within 30 days; if anything remains, we will tell you what and how it will be handled.

The full steps are on our Data Deletion Instructions page. We do not operate an automatic deletion service.
